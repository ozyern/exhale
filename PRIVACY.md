# Privacy policy

Exhale has no account of its own, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Nothing about you is
sent to the developer. Your settings, library, listening history and downloads stay on your device
(on Windows, in `%APPDATA%\Exhale`).

To play and describe music, Exhale connects directly to these services. Each one receives what it
needs to answer, plus the usual details any connection carries (your IP address, for one), and its own
privacy policy applies.

| Service | What it's used for | What it receives | Can it be turned off? |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube Music / YouTube | Search, streaming, recommendations; your library and playlists if you sign in | Your searches and the songs you play; your Google sign-in cookies, if you sign in | Signing in is optional |
| Lyrics sources (LRCLIB, KuGou, SimpMusic, BetterLyrics and similar) | Synced lyrics | The title, artist and length of the playing song | Lyrics settings |
| Apple Music, Tidal | Moving album covers | The title and artist of the playing song | Moving covers setting |
| Internet Archive, Jamendo, Wikimedia Commons | The FLAC page's catalogue and streams | What you browse and play on that page | Only used on the FLAC page |
| GitHub | Checking for new versions of Exhale | The version you have | Settings › Updates |
| Last.fm, ListenBrainz | Scrobbling | The songs you play, with your account token | Off unless you sign in to them |
| Discord | Showing what you're playing on your profile | The playing song, with your Discord token | Off unless you turn it on |

Sign-in details for YouTube Music, Last.fm, ListenBrainz and Discord are stored only on your device and
are sent only to the service they belong to.

## Uninstalling

On Windows, *Uninstall Exhale* (in Settings › Apps › Installed apps) removes the app, and can also
remove everything in `%APPDATA%\Exhale`. On Android, uninstalling the app removes its data.
