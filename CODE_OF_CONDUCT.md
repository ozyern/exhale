# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Exhale to be a friendly, harassment-free project for everyone, whatever their age, background, identity, experience or ability. We pledge to act and interact in ways that keep the community open, welcoming and respectful.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Disagree politely and be open to being wrong.
- Keep discussion on topic. Bug reports and pull requests are not the place for unrelated arguments.
- Assume good intent, and say clearly when something is unclear.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Spam, advertising, or repeatedly pinging people for attention.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break them. Repeated or serious problems can lead to a temporary or permanent ban from the project.

## Reporting

To report a problem, open a private [security advisory](https://github.com/ozyern/exhale/security/advisories/new) if it involves safety. Reports are kept private, and we will look into each one and respond.
